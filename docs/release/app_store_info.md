## Description

```text
A calm, private migraine tracker for people whose attacks follow the weather - especially a falling barometric pressure.

LOG IN THREE TAPS
• Intensity, head location, medication. Done.
• Works fully offline - no signal needed.
• A running attack gets a live timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
• Note when a dose was taken and when the pain eased.

SEE YOUR WEATHER LINK
• Every attack is paired with the pressure at that moment.
• Attacks sit on the pressure curve at the hour they started.
• See what share of your attacks lined up with rapid pressure drops.
• Bring the pattern to your doctor as a PDF report.

GET AHEAD OF A PRESSURE DROP (PREMIUM)
• A push before pressure is forecast to fall past your threshold.
• A second push as the fall begins.
• Five days of pressure behind the forecast chart.

A 30-SECOND DAILY CHECK-IN
• Sleep, stress, water, screen time and more - attack days and quiet days alike.
• Builds a trigger and protector map from your own history.
• Feeds a rule-based risk score for the week ahead, with weights you can read.
• MIDAS questionnaire, scored, included in the doctor report.

ONE TAP FROM YOUR HOME SCREEN
• Widget with the log button, this week's count and the latest pressure.
• Siri opens the log flow or the daily check-in.

BUILT FOR SENSITIVE EYES
• Dark by default. No harsh white screens, no flashing.
• Designed for photophobia.

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
• Local-first: your history lives on your phone.
• Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to sign in and sync.
• No ads, no tracking.
• Coarse (~city-scale) location only, and only while using the app.
• Apple Health sleep, steps and cycle are read on-device.
• Export as JSON or CSV, or delete everything - account included - from Settings.

PREMIUM UNLOCKS
• Pressure-drop push alerts
• Pressure forecast chart for the week
• Trigger, protector and factor analysis
• Weekly risk score
• MIDAS questionnaire and score
• PDF doctor report
• Apple Health sleep correlation

FREE PLAN
• 40 logged attacks, 5 medications, 2 reminders.
• Premium lifts those limits, monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.
• Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.

SUBSCRIPTION TERMS
• Premium Monthly and Premium Yearly (7-day free trial) are auto-renewable subscriptions.
• Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
• A subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
• Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of that period.
• Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings after purchase.
• Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
• Privacy Policy: https://damhongduc.github.io/personal_work_space/apps/baro-ease/privacy_policy/

Medical disclaimer: BaroEase is a self-tracking tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Always consult a qualified clinician.
```
